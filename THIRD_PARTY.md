# Third-party notices

## The engine

`engine/` began as a copy of an existing Go GUI toolkit and is maintained here.
It is distributed under **Unlicense OR MIT**, and the notice travelled with it:
`engine/LICENSE`. The compiled shader programs under `engine/shader` came from
that project's shader module, under the same terms, with the notice in
`engine/shader/LICENSE`.

`engine/UPSTREAM.md` records the exact version, what diverges on purpose, and
how a fix published upstream is ported here.

## Design tokens

`theme/basecoat/base.css` is vendored under MIT, with the notice beside it in
`theme/basecoat/LICENSE.md`. Only the stylesheet travelled: it is the source
the palette is generated from, so that a colour named the same thing on both
halves of the product is the same colour.
